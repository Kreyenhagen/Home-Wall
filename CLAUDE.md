# Home Wall

Android tablet app + ESP32 firmware for a home climbing wall with WS2811 LEDs behind the holds,
controlled over BLE. GitHub: https://github.com/Kreyenhagen/Home-Wall

The north star doc below is the project's source of truth. Follow its §0 working rules
(milestone order, stop after each milestone, ask before changing the doc or deviating from it).
Track progress in `PROGRESS.md` at the repo root.
